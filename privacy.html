Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This is a template privacy policy for demonstration purposes. Before operating a real visa assistance service, have this reviewed by a qualified lawyer to ensure compliance with applicable data protection laws (such as GDPR, CCPA, or local equivalents) in every jurisdiction you operate in.

1. Information we collect

To process a visa application, we collect personal information including your full name, date of birth, nationality, passport number and expiry date, contact details, travel plans, and copies of identity documents and photographs you upload.

2. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to prepare, review and submit your visa application, to communicate with you about its status, to provide customer support, and to comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

3. How we share your information

We share your application information with the relevant government or embassy authority responsible for issuing your visa. We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers who help us operate this website, under confidentiality obligations.

4. Data retention

We retain application data for as long as necessary to process your application and meet legal, tax or regulatory retention requirements, after which it is securely deleted.

5. Document security

Passport scans and photographs are sensitive documents. We restrict access to authorized personnel and use encryption in transit and at rest. In this demonstration version of the site, uploaded files are never transmitted anywhere — they remain only in your browser session.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. Contact us at privacy@onlinevisastore.com to exercise these rights.

7. Cookies

This site may use cookies or browser storage to remember your progress through the application form and to keep you signed in to a session. We do not use this data for third-party advertising.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@onlinevisastore.com or via our contact form.
