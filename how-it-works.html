How It Works

From choosing your destination to receiving your visa, here's what to expect at every stage.

1

Choose your destination and visa type

Search our countries and pricing page to find your destination, confirm the visa type you need (tourist, business or transit) and check the processing time and service fee.

2

Complete the online application

Fill in your traveler details — name, date of birth, nationality, passport number and contact information — exactly as they appear on your passport. Our form validates entries as you go so you catch mistakes early.

3

Upload your documents

Provide a clear scan of your passport's photo page and a recent passport-style photo. We recommend well-lit, uncropped scans in JPG, PNG or PDF format.

4

Review and submit

Double-check your details on the review screen, agree to our terms, and submit. You'll receive a unique reference number to track your application.

5

Track your status

Use the Track Application page with your reference number and email to follow your application through document review, processing and approval.

6

Receive your visa

Once approved, your visa or entry authorization is sent to the email on file. Some destinations issue an electronic visa; others may require your passport to be stamped on arrival — we'll tell you which applies to your destination.

Most applications take about 10 minutes to complete.
