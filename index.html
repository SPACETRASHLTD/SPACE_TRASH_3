Frequently asked

Before you book.

How can I book a reading?

Consultations and readings are available in person (by appointment or walk-in), by phone, on Zoom, or as a written PDF report. Bookings are concierge-based, so the quickest way to schedule is to call +1 (825) 977-9906. You can also request a slot through the form below and we’ll respond with availability as soon as possible.

How helpful is astrology, really?

When it’s done predictively and with real technique — very. Vedic astrology in particular uses the sidereal zodiac and the Vimshottari Dasha system to map specific timelines for career shifts, relationship changes, financial gains, and challenges. The point isn’t to be told a story; the point is to know when something is most likely so you can act on it.

What information do you need from me?

For a chart-based reading: your date of birth, exact time of birth, and place of birth. Time is the most important — check your birth certificate if you can. For a tarot or psychic session, just bring the question.

Is this confidential?

Completely. Nothing said in session is shared, recorded, or repeated. Readings are a judgment-free space — you can bring anything.

How long is a session?

Most live sessions run 45–75 minutes depending on the format and the depth you’re after. Written PDF reports are delivered within 5–7 days of booking.
