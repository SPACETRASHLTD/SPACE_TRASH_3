Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about applying for a visa through OnlineVisaStore.

Most applications require a scan of your passport's photo page and a recent passport-style photo. Some destinations or visa types may require additional documents such as proof of onward travel or accommodation — we'll let you know if your destination needs anything extra.

Processing times vary by destination, from as little as 24-48 hours for electronic travel authorizations to 10-15 business days for some embassy-processed visas. Check the specific processing time on our Countries & Pricing page before you apply.

Our service fee depends on the destination and is shown upfront on the Countries & Pricing page. This fee covers application review and submission assistance. Some destinations also charge a separate official government visa fee, which we'll disclose clearly before you pay.
